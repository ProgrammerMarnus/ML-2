WHAT THE 9 pv-2.2.0 FEATURES CHANGED - ATTRIBUTION HYPOTHESES:
================================================================================

1. OVERNIGHT/INTRADAY DECOMPOSITION (overnight_gap, intraday_return):
   The biggest likely contributor. Overnight returns mean-revert while daytime
   returns trend differently; separating them gives the model two independent
   edges instead of one noisy daily return. Fold 2 (2019-20) going from 0 to
   24 profitable trades suggests the model now sees regime-appropriate signal
   where legacy features were blind.

2. RANGE/LOCATION FEATURES (day_range_position, bollinger_position_20,
   fifty_two_week_position): closing location within the day/52wk range is a
   classic continuation/reversal tell. Likely improved the 2022 bear fold
   (whipsaw identification).

3. RSI_14: classic momentum oscillator - complements the legacy rolling
   momentum with a bounded, normalized form.

4. VOLATILITY CLUSTERING (vol_of_vol_20): identifies vol-regime transitions,
   which matter for the vol-targeted sizing.

5. LIQUIDITY/VOLUME INTERACTION (price_volume_corr_20, amihud_illiquidity_20):
   volume-confirmation and liquidity stress add an information dimension
   absent from pure price features.
