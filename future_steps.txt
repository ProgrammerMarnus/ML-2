FUTURE STEPS — INSTITUTIONAL QUANT RESEARCH ENGINE
As reviewed: 2026-09-13

PROJECT READ
The research platform has substantial point-in-time, walk-forward, cost and
experiment-history controls. Its current pv-2.2.0 strategy evidence is
promising but not sufficient for promotion. DISCOVERY_ANALYSIS.txt records
eight fresh-download runs: the predeclared placebo-separation gate failed in
all eight, while a historical CANDIDATE result has not been reproduced. The
same-seed results also move with refreshed market data. Keep the current
strategy at RESEARCH_ONLY. Do not keep changing seeds or relax gates to obtain
a pass.

IMPLEMENTATION STATUS — 2026-09-13
V2.1.5 implements and regression-tests configurable fill latency,
cross-symbol pending-cash reservation, fill-time cash/exposure validation,
conflicting order-ID rejection, reduce-only emergency exits, unique processed
session counting, research-record binding, and observed-paper evidence gates.
It also persists verified broker snapshots and rejects a restored state with a
tampered audit chain or unreconciled positions.
These engineering fixes do not change the current strategy's RESEARCH_ONLY
status.

RECOMMENDED ORDER

1. Close the current strategy search line and preserve its evidence.
   Keep the original experiment records, raw snapshots, configs, and the
   composite comparison together. Record pv-2.2.0 as a completed research
   result with its unresolved placebo failure. Treat the historical seed-42
   CANDIDATE as an unreplicated result, not the current status.
   Done when: the registry/report clearly identifies the frozen strategy,
   exact dataset hashes, gate outcomes, and the decision not to promote.

2. Require a new, testable hypothesis before the next strategy experiment.
   State the mechanism, expected effect, features, target, costs, and one
   primary success criterion before running it. Separate hypothesis
   development data from a genuinely untouched evaluation period or asset
   set. Count every attempted variant in the existing research-family
   accounting. Do not select a hypothesis by repeatedly inspecting the same
   OOS period.
   Done when: a short preregistration and frozen config exist before results
   are generated, and evaluation data cannot be used for candidate selection.
   Implementation: `quant_research.experiments.protocol.ResearchProtocol`
   creates a write-once, digest-verified protocol that is optionally attached
   through `research.protocol_path`. A future trading hypothesis still needs
   to be chosen and supplied by the researcher.

3. Reconcile the latest formal audit findings against the current code, then
   fix any still-open P1 items before using discovery or paper evidence.
   DEEP_AUDIT_2026-09-11.md found serious issues in nested-discovery replay
   costs and locked-test enforcement, paper-session promotion, safety/breach
   evidence, kill-switch handling, liquidation, pending-order exposure,
   latency, and order-ID retries. That audit covered an earlier commit, so
   first verify each counterexample against the current branch rather than
   assuming every finding is still present. Fix the remaining issues in this
   order: (a) discovery cost reconciliation and locked-test enforcement;
   (b) paper execution ledger and emergency controls; (c) paper-validation
   evidence and promotion gates.
   Done when: each applicable audit counterexample has a regression test and
   a no-override nested replay matches positions, turnover, costs, and net
   returns; paper validation cannot advance from repeated function calls.

4. Establish a trustworthy current test baseline.
   The 2026-09-11 audit reported 308 passing tests, 6 failures, one teardown
   error, and shared research-state pollution. Rerun the suite on the current
   branch in an isolated temporary copy or isolated state directory. Repair
   any remaining stale assertions, estimator-specific assumptions, and
   shared-ledger contamination before using the suite as release evidence.
   Done when: the full suite passes repeatably without modifying project
   artifacts or leaking state between tests, and the run log is retained.

5. Improve data-version reproducibility before comparing future results.
   Recent analysis shows refreshed yfinance data can change results for the
   same seed and config. Use saved, hash-verified snapshots for controlled
   comparisons; report snapshot hashes alongside metrics; test provider
   refresh sensitivity as a separate robustness question. Keep code/config
   identity and all search attempts attached to each result.
   Done when: a replay from a saved snapshot reproduces the recorded ledger,
   and a refresh comparison is clearly distinguished from a seed comparison.

6. Defer paper trading until research and execution evidence are credible.
   The 2026-09-11 audit found that paper readiness could be overstated and
   that key order-flow controls were incomplete. First make fills causal and
   idempotent, maintain cash/position reconciliation, validate fresh marks,
   and ensure emergency liquidation works. Then require unique real or
   explicitly simulated trading sessions, event-derived breach counts, and
   durable audit evidence. Do not treat the current simulator as evidence of
   live operational readiness.
   Done when: failure/restart, kill-switch, liquidation, partial-fill,
   duplicate-request, stale-price, and session-duration scenarios pass in
   end-to-end tests, with no live adapter implied by simulator results.

7. Only after these foundations, investigate the signal's placebo failure.
   Use the current placebo method and thresholds as predeclared. Ask whether
   the signal has a defensible economic mechanism and whether the null is
   appropriate for the feature/target dependence structure. If the hypothesis
   changes, create a new version and account for it as a new search family;
   do not tune features or gates against the existing OOS results.
   Done when: a new hypothesis beats the predeclared null and all cost,
   delay, bootstrap, fold-dominance, integrity, and trial-accounting gates on
   untouched evidence.

NOT RECOMMENDED NOW
More seed-only reruns of pv-2.2.0, promoting from the historical anchor,
changing acceptance thresholds to fit observed results, or starting paper
trading before the paper-execution and validation defects are resolved.
